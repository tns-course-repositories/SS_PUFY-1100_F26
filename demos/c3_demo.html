🧰 System Behavior → Graphic Behavior

📍 Introduction

Complex systems are often difficult to perceive directly.

They may be too large, too slow, invisible, interconnected, or distributed across space and time.

Design can make these relationships legible.

The examples in this demo show how artists, scientists, cartographers, designers, and researchers have developed graphic strategies for representing complex phenomena.

Use These as Tools, Not Templates

Do not simply copy the appearance of these examples.

Instead, ask:

  • What behavior is being represented?
  • What graphic operation makes that behavior visible?
  • Could that operation help communicate your system?

These examples are resources for both Part I — Experiment and Part II — Develop.

◼️ Apply

From Precedent → Experiment

Do not ask:

What should my drawing look like?

Ask:

What does my system do?

Then identify a graphic operation that might make that behavior visible.

System Behavior Possible Graphic Operations
Connection line · point · intersection
Accumulation repetition · density · growth
Feedback loop · return · recurrence
Threshold boundary · crossing · contrast
Time / Acceleration sequence · spacing · displacement
Interdependence network · branching · overlap
Scale nesting · hierarchy · magnification
