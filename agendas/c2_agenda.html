4:10pm – 4:25pm | Guided Reading Discussion

Underland | Robert Macfarlane

  1. What does Macfarlane mean by the “underland”? What kinds of things do humans hide, preserve, extract, or leave behind underground?

  2. What is “deep time”? How does thinking across geological time change how we understand the things humans build and do today?

  3. What will we leave behind? If someone encountered the physical traces of our civilization far in the future, what might those traces reveal about how we lived?

Dymaxion Map | Buckminster Fuller Institute

  1. Fuller wanted the Dymaxion Map to show Earth as “one island in one ocean.” How might changing the representation of the Earth change the way we understand relationships between places? What does the Dymaxion Map make easier to see than a conventional world map?
