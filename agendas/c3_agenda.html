💬 4:10pm – 4:25pm | Guided Reading Discussion

CONCEPT GROUPS

14 students → 5 groups

Working with your group, prepare a short explanation of your assigned concept.

You have 7–10 minutes to discuss the concept and prepare a 1–2 minute explanation for the class.

Your explanation should address each of the required components below.

GROUP 1 — CLIMATE CRISIS

INCLUDE
  • What distinguishes climate from individual weather events?
  • How does fossil-fuel energy contribute to changes in the Earth system?
  • Why does the reading describe climate disruption as a problem that operates across space + time?
CONSIDER

Why is climate change difficult to perceive as a single event or object?

GROUP 2 — METHANE

INCLUDE
  • What is methane and why is it important as a greenhouse gas?
  • What are fugitive emissions, and where do they occur within energy infrastructure?
  • Why does time matter when considering methane’s climate impact?
CONSIDER

How does methane connect an individual piece of energy infrastructure to the larger climate system?

GROUP 3 — TIPPING POINTS + HOTHOUSE EARTH

INCLUDE
  • What is a tipping point?
  • Why is climate change not necessarily a linear process?
  • What are the two possible trajectories represented by Stabilized Earth and Hothouse Earth?
CONSIDER

What happens when change alters the behavior of the system itself?

GROUP 4 — ENERGY IS SPATIAL

INCLUDE
  • What does it mean to say that energy is spatial?
  • How are energy systems organized through nodes, flows, corridors, and networks?
  • What is an energy vector, and how does it connect otherwise distant places?
CONSIDER

Where does an energy system begin—and where does it end?

GROUP 5 — ENERGY HAZARDSCAPES

INCLUDE
  • What is an energy hazardscape?
  • How can multiple forms of energy infrastructure and risk accumulate within the same landscape?
  • Why are location + proximity + time important for understanding a hazardscape?
CONSIDER

How can infrastructure transform a landscape over time?

REPORT BACK

Each group has 1–2 minutes to explain its concept.

Do not summarize the entire reading.

Identify the idea → explain how it works → tell us why it matters.

As each group reports, listen for connections between the concepts:

ENERGY → SPACE → INFRASTRUCTURE → EMISSIONS → CLIMATE → EARTH SYSTEM
