Class 11 Agenda Items

Class 11 | Recycling, Recovery & Circular Design

How can discarded materials be recovered, transformed, and designed for continued use?

  • This class examines urban recycling infrastructure through a virtual tour of the Sunset Park Material Recovery Facility, followed by a collaborative lab exploring waste as a material resource.

  • There is no Assignment 11 submission this week; however there is a required reading that will be quizzed next week.

Class 10 Reading Quiz | Biomimicry

  • Class 10 Reading Quiz - Biomimicry, covering the reading assigned in Class 10 Assignment.
  • Access via Canvas Reading Quiz Section.
  • Quiz Access Code: b8k2

Virtual Tour | Sunset Park Material Recovery Facility

4:00 pm - 5:00 pm
  • Attendance at the start of the session.
  • One-hour virtual visit with the Sunset Park Material Recovery Facility (Recycling Education Center).
  • Explore material collection, sorting, recovery, and recycling operations through photos, videos, and graphics.
  • Questions and answers with facility educators.

Break

5:00 pm - 5:10 pm
  • Short Break

Class 11 Lecture & Lab Introduction | Waste, Recovery & Circular Design

5:20 pm - 5:35 pm
  • Class 11 Lecture
  • Short slide presentation introducing waste streams, material recovery, circular design, and design for disassembly.
  • Introduce the Waste-to-Resource Design Challenge, including available materials, constraints, and expected outcomes.
  • No lecture quiz for Class 11.

Class 11 Assignment | Waste-to-Resource Design Challenge

5:35 pm - 6:15 pm
  • Class 11 Assignment
  • Work collaboratively in small groups to transform clean, recovered materials into a useful or expressive object.
  • Explore material properties, structural connections, reuse, and the possibility of future disassembly and recovery.
  • Prepare a brief explanation of the group’s design decisions.

Group Presentations | Material Transformation & Recovery

6:15 pm - 6:35 pm
  • Each group presents its object and explains its source materials, construction strategy, and potential for reuse or disassembly.
  • Discuss how design choices influence whether materials remain useful or become waste.

Wrap-Up | Reflection

6:35 pm - 6:40 pm
  • Connect the industrial-scale recycling processes observed during the tour to the small-scale design decisions made in the lab.
  • No separate out-of-class assignment for this Class 11 week.
