Class 12 Agenda Items

Class 12 | From Materials to Systems: Final Project Development

How can creative investigation reveal, challenge, or reimagine the systems that shape our material world?

  • Introduce earth-derived chemistry and material alternatives in preparation for next week’s paint-making lab.
  • Revisit the semester’s key themes and investigations, then introduce the final project and begin developing individual project directions.
  • Class 12 Assignment includes in-class exercises and a final project proposal due Sunday, November 22, at 11:59 p.m.

Class 11 Reading Quiz | Material Transformation & Creative Practice

4:00 pm - 4:10 pm
  • Class 11 Reading Quiz, covering:
    • Tara Donovan — Hammer Projects: Tara Donovan
    • El Anatsui — Ideas and Creativity Are Not Restricted to Any One Place
  • Access via Canvas Reading Quiz Section.
  • Quiz Access Code: W5y%

Class 12 Lecture | Earth → Chemistry → Materials

4:10 pm - 4:25 pm
  • Conceptual introduction to the November 19 paint-making lab: natural pigments, binders, and the chemistry of earth-derived materials.
  • Consider the potential and limitations of mineral- and bio-based alternatives to fossil-fuel-derived materials.
  • Connect to earlier investigations of bioplastics, biomimicry, and healthy materials.
  • Link to Lecture 12A - Earth > Chemistry > Materials

Review of Class 11 Submissions | Waste-to-Resource Design Challenge

  • Briefly review the four group projects produced during the Class 11 design challenge.
  • Revisit how each group transformed recovered materials and addressed material properties, assembly, reuse, or disassembly.
  • Connect the projects to the broader question of how material decisions shape sustainable design.
  • LINK to Submissions

Semester Overview | Underland, Energy & Making

4:25 pm - 4:40 pm (includes submissions review)
  • Revisit the three principal thematic pathways developed through the semester:
    • Underland: Material origins, extraction, and deep time.
    • Energy: Systems, infrastructure, environmental impacts, and justice.
    • Making: Material choices, transformation, experimentation, and alternatives.
  • Recall key lectures, films, fieldwork, and labs as possible starting points for final project investigations.
  • Reconnect to course methods: Observe → Research → Map → Make → Communicate.
  • Link to Lecture 12B - Semester + Final Project Overivew

Final Project Introduction | Scope, Process & Expectations

4:40 pm - 5:00 pm
  • Introduce the final project brief, thematic parameters, and possible creative formats.
  • Review the process from ideation and selection through research, development, iteration, and presentation.
  • Clarify research expectations, deliverables, evaluation criteria, and remaining deadlines.
  • Discuss brief hypothetical examples showing topic → question → creative investigation; these are not models of finished work.

Class 12 Assignment | Final Project Ideation & Proposal

5:00 pm - 6:30 pm
  • Exercise 1 — Project Ideation (5:00–5:20): Generate three possible project directions connected to course themes; discuss possibilities with a peer.
  • Exercise 2 — From Idea to System (5:20–5:40): Select one direction, develop a focused question, and sketch a preliminary systems diagram.
  • Exercise 3 — Peer Review (5:40–6:00): Share the emerging concept and receive structured feedback on clarity, relevance, creative possibilities, and feasibility.
  • Project Development & Instructor Consultations (6:00–6:30): Refine the project direction, proposed creative format, research needs, and next steps.
  • Complete the Class 12 Assignment with a developed Final Project Proposal, due Sunday, November 22, 2026, at 11:59 p.m.

Wrap-Up | Reflection & Next Steps

6:30 pm - 6:40 pm
  • Review the preliminary concept sheets and systems diagrams developed during class.
  • Clarify the November 19 paint-making lab and how material experimentation may inform final project ideas.
  • Review the November 22 proposal deadline and the subsequent project development and final presentation schedule.
