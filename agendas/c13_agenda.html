Class 13 Agenda Items

Class 13 | Paint Making Lab (Lead by Prof. Metts & Maizie Lee)

How can earth-derived pigments and binders be transformed into usable paints through material experimentation?

  • Explore paint as a material system composed of pigment, binder, and liquid.
  • Prepare, mix, and test small batches of handmade paints using the provided recipes and materials.
  • Observe how changes in ingredients and proportions affect color, consistency, coverage, and application.
  • No Class 13 assignment. The Class 12 Final Project Proposal remains due Sunday, November 22, at 11:59 p.m.

Class Introduction | Paint Making Process

4:00 pm - 4:20 pm | Room L101
  • Introduce the goals, sequence, and working process for the paint-making lab.
  • Review the roles of pigments, binders, and liquids in paint formulations.
  • Explain the recipes, preparation steps, testing methods, and expectations for safe material handling.
  • Class 13 Slides
  • Paint Recipes & Instructions

Transition | Bio Lab Orientation & Setup

4:20 pm - 4:45 pm | Move to Room 903
  • Walk together from the regular classroom to Bio Lab Room 903.
  • Review lab procedures, materials, tools, workstations, and cleanup expectations.
  • Organize into working groups and prepare materials for mixing and testing.

Paint Making Lab | Mixing, Testing & Experimentation

4:45 pm - 6:25 pm | Room 903
  • Follow the paint recipes to prepare small batches using provided materials.
  • Experiment with pigment-to-binder ratios and observe differences in paint behavior.
  • Apply paints to sample watercolor paper and compare color, opacity, texture, and drying characteristics.
  • Record observations and consider how material choices affect both creative possibilities and environmental impacts.
  • Students may use their own watercolor or gouache brushes and materials alongside those provided.

Lab Cleanup | Paint Collection & Closing Discussion

6:25 pm - 6:40 pm | Room 903
  • Transfer usable paint samples into take-home containers.
  • Clean tools and workstations according to lab procedures.
  • Briefly share observations about paint performance and material experimentation.
  • Reminder: Class 12 Final Project Proposal due Sunday, November 22, 2026, at 11:59 p.m.
