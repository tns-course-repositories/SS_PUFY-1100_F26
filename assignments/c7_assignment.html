🧭 Assignment 7 — Bioplastics Lab Reflection

💡 Introduction

Class 7 — Thursday | 10-08-26 — features a bioplastics lab led by CA Maizie Lee.

In the lab, we will experiment with making bioplastics from biologically derived ingredients and consider how alternative materials relate to larger systems of production, use, and disposal.

Use the lab to make, observe, question, and connect. This assignment is a short reflection rather than a formal laboratory report.

📝 BIOPLASTICS LAB REFLECTION

After the lab, prepare a 300–400 word reflection responding to the following four prompts. Include 1–2 photographs documenting your process or material.

01 — MATERIAL + PROCESS

What did you observe while making your bioplastic?

Describe the ingredients, process, and resulting material. Identify something that surprised you.

02 — PERFORMANCE + LIMITATIONS

How might your bioplastic compare with conventional plastic?

Consider strength, flexibility, durability, or possible uses. What limitations did you observe or anticipate? Distinguish what you tested from what you are predicting.

03 — SYSTEMS + SUSTAINABILITY

Does a biologically derived material necessarily make a product sustainable?

Consider at least two factors beyond the material itself, such as agriculture, energy, water, transportation, or end-of-life disposal.

04 — REFLECTION

How did this experiment change the way you think about materials?

Connect one insight from the lab to the broader environmental consequences of material choices.

Your submission should:
