🧭 Assignment 6 — Governors Island Site Visit Report

💡 Introduction

Class 6 takes place on Governors Island.

Our field trip will use the island as a site for exploring relationships between art, climate, ecology, and resilience.

Our group — Group 3: Climate Resilience and Art — will visit:

  • Billion Oyster Project — exploring oysters, New York Harbor, ecological restoration, and climate resilience.
  • Climate Imaginarium — exploring climate change through art, artists, and creative practice.

Use the field trip to observe, ask questions, and identify what stays with you.

📝 SITE VISIT REPORT

After the field trip, prepare a brief 1-page site visit report responding to the following three prompts.

01 — SURPRISE

What surprised you?

Identify one thing you encountered during the field trip that you did not expect.

Briefly explain what surprised you and why.

02 — INVESTIGATE

What would you like to investigate further?

Identify one idea, artwork, material, system, place, or question from the trip that you would like to know more about.

Briefly explain what interests you about it.

03 — COMPELLING

What did you find most compelling?

Identify one experience, artwork, place, idea, or observation that stayed with you after the trip.

Briefly explain why.

🔧 SUBMIT TO CANVAS

Submit one PDF file to Canvas.

GOVERNORS ISLAND SITE VISIT REPORT

Your report should:

  • be approximately 1 page
  • address all three prompts
  • clearly identify the places, works, systems, or ideas you discuss
